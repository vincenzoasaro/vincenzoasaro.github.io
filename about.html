About me

I'm a training and instructional design professional based in Sicily, working remotely with international teams.

From the floor to the training room

At ModSquad I started as a support agent, moved on to become a curator (the team's subject-matter expert), and then joined the training team as a team lead. That's the standard path there: every team lead, APM and PM starts on the support floor. Each step changed how I train: I know what new agents worry about, because I worried about the same things.

Across about twelve years of work, I've built onboarding programs, curricula and reporting systems, and I hold a bachelor's degree in business. Support teams are where I've applied it most recently, but the methods behind it, from needs analysis and instructional design to facilitation and evaluation, work for any subject and any audience.

Outside of training

I host an Italian history podcast, Squarci di Storia, and write an economics education blog at vincenzoasaro.it. Both come from the same habit: taking something complicated and making it clear.
